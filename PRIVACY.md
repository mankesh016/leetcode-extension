# Privacy Policy — LeetCode Streak Dot Fixer

Last updated: October 7, 2026

LeetCode Streak Dot Fixer runs only on leetcode.com.

What it accesses

- The daily-challenge calendar shown on leetcode.com pages.
- Your daily-challenge solve status and LeetCode user ID, fetched from leetcode.com using your existing login.

What it stores

- The dates of daily challenges you have solved, when each month was last checked, and your LeetCode user ID.
- This is stored only in your browser (chrome.storage.local) and never leaves your device.

What it shares

- Nothing. The extension sends no data to the developer or any third party. Its only network requests go to leetcode.com.
- No analytics, tracking, ads, or cookies of its own.

Removing your data

- Uninstalling the extension deletes all stored data.

Contact

- Open an issue at [/issues](https://github.com/mankesh016/leetcode-extension/issues)

This extension is not affiliated with or endorsed by LeetCode.
